The Design North Star

Primary Design Persona — VP Sales / Sales Leader

A VP Sales arrives each morning with five implicit questions. They do not browse dashboards. They do not read feeds. They need answers — fast, trusted, and actionable. Every element of the DeepMindQ experience must be filtered through this lens:

"Would a VP Sales trust this, rely on this daily, and recommend this to their team?"

Behavioral Principles

schedule
Time-Scarce Decision Maker
Has 15 minutes max for intelligence review. Needs signal, not noise.
verified_user
Trust-Sensitive Executive

trending_up
Action-Oriented Leader
Intelligence without a recommended next step is wasted cognitive load.
The 5 Morning Questions
1 What changed?
2 Why does it matter?
3 Who should we engage?
4 What should we say?
5 What should we do?
The VP Sales mental model is not about demographics or job titles. It is about cognitive patterns. This persona represents anyone who is time-scarce, trust-sensitive, and action-oriented. If we serve this mindset, we serve all power users. The 5 morning questions become the structural backbone of the entire Intelligence Hub.
