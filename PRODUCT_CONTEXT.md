# DeepMindQ — Product Context (Permanent)

## Product Identity

**DeepMindQ is an Enterprise AI Intelligence Platform designed for enterprise deployment/licensing.**

## What DeepMindQ IS

- Intelligence capabilities platform
- Signal intelligence
- Account intelligence
- AI research and enrichment
- Multi-factor scoring
- Evidence-backed recommendations
- Knowledge intelligence
- AI-assisted sales intelligence
- Revenue intelligence
- Enterprise deployment / enterprise licensing model

## What DeepMindQ is NOT

- DeepMindQ is NOT a CRM
- DeepMindQ is NOT a SaaS self-service application
- DeepMindQ is NOT a CRM replacement
- DeepMindQ is NOT a pipeline management tool
- DeepMindQ is NOT a contact database product
- DeepMindQ is NOT a generic SaaS application
- DeepMindQ is NOT a self-service subscription platform

## Intentionally OUT OF SCOPE

Unless specifically required for an enterprise deployment model, these are NOT requirements:

1. Multi-tenancy (single-tenant enterprise deployment is the model)
2. Subscription billing (enterprise licensing)
3. CRM workflow engines (integration target, not built-in)
4. Sales pipeline management (customer's CRM handles this)
5. Customer self-service onboarding (vendor-managed deployment)

## Audit & Evaluation Rules

All audits, scoring, and recommendations MUST use this product definition.

**The correct audit question is:**
> "Is DeepMindQ production-ready as an Enterprise AI Intelligence Platform?"

**NOT:**
> "Is DeepMindQ production-ready as a multi-tenant CRM SaaS?"

## Priority Order

1. Security confidence
2. Testing confidence
3. AI accuracy confidence
4. Deployment confidence
5. UI/UX confidence
6. Enterprise documentation

## Guiding Principle

DeepMindQ already has the intelligence foundation.
The next stage is NOT building more capability.
The next stage is proving that the capability is reliable, secure, and enterprise-ready.
