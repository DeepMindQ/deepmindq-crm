The 5-Question Morning Intelligence Protocol

Every element of the Intelligence Hub maps to these five questions

notification_important

1. What Changed?

Surface critical signals — account movements, market shifts, competitive changes, opportunity updates. Ranked by impact, not recency.

lightbulb

2. Why It Matters

Context and reasoning behind every signal. Impact analysis, trend correlation, revenue implication.

people

3. Who to Engage

Account-specific recommendations. Priority-ranked engagement targets with reasoning.

chat

4. What to Say

Conversation angles, talking points, and contextual intelligence for each engagement.

rocket_launch

5. What to Do

Recommended actions with confidence scores, timelines, and evidence trails.

DESIGN RULE

No intelligence element exists in the Hub that does not map to at least one of these five questions. If a component cannot answer which question it serves, it belongs in a deeper experience layer, not the Hub.

The 5-Question Framework is the structural backbone of the Intelligence Hub. It is not a feature — it is an information architecture constraint. Every card, signal, recommendation, and action in the Hub must map to one of these five questions. This prevents the Hub from becoming an overloaded dashboard.
