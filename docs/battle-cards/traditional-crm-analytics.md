# Battle Card: Traditional CRM Analytics

## Competitor Profile
CRM-native analytics and reporting tools, including Salesforce Reports/Dashboards,
HubSpot Sales Analytics, and third-party CRM add-ons (e.g., Datadog for CRM).

## DeepMindQ Position
CRM analytics tells you what's IN your pipeline. DeepMindQ tells you what's
MISSING from your pipeline — and where to find it. We operate on external
intelligence about your target market, not internal pipeline data.

## Key Differentiators

### 1. External Intelligence vs. Internal Reporting
- **We**: Analyze 7+ external data sources about target companies (funding,
  hiring signals, SEC filings, tech stack changes, news).
- **CRM Analytics**: Only sees what's already in your CRM — contact history,
  deal stage, activity metrics.

### 2. Predictive vs. Historical
- **We**: Predictive signals (hiring spikes suggest budget expansion, SEC
  filings reveal M&A activity, tech stack changes indicate vendor evaluation).
- **CRM Analytics**: Historical reporting (won/lost rates, cycle times,
  conversion funnels).

### 3. Company-Size Agnostic Intelligence
- **We**: Our intelligence engine handles any company from Google to Joe's
  Plumbing — different signals matter at different scales, and we know which.
- **CRM Analytics**: Typically optimized for mid-market+ sales processes.

### 4. Actionable Recommendations
- **We**: Don't just show data — generate prioritized recommendations with
  evidence-backed reasoning, risk factors, and suggested conversation angles.
- **CRM Analytics**: Requires humans to interpret dashboards and draw conclusions.

## Proof Points
- Signal detection across 7+ external sources
- Temporal intelligence tracking (velocity, freshness, growth trends)
- Intelligence Maturity Index per company
- Data Depth Indicators on every recommendation
- Full audit trail for compliance

## Common Objections & Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We can build reports in Salesforce" | Salesforce shows you your pipeline. DeepMindQ shows you the market OUTSIDE your pipeline — companies you should be targeting but aren't. |
| "We have BI tools (Tableau/Looker) already" | BI tools visualize data you already have. DeepMindQ DISCOVERS new data about your targets from external sources. |
| "Our CRM analytics are free/included" | What's the cost of missed opportunities because you didn't know a target company just raised $50M or is hiring for your exact capability? |

## Win Themes
1. "See outside your pipeline"
2. "Intelligence, not just information"
3. "Know the market, not just your CRM"
