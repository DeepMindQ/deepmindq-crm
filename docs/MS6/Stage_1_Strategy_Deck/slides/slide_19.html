“We are not removing intelligence. We are making intelligence usable.”

This is the most important slide in the deck. It shows the transformation visually and concretely. The current Executive Dashboard has 12 KPI cards, activity feeds, pipeline stages, and audit logs — but a VP Sales looking at it cannot immediately answer: what matters? What changed? What should I do? Why trust this? The Intelligence Hub transforms this from a data display into an executive briefing. The same intelligence exists — but it is now structured, prioritized, and actionable. We are not removing intelligence. We are making intelligence usable.
