navigation

Navigation Intelligence

The nav itself answers: what should I focus on next? (Design Pattern #4)

This is the new IA. The Intelligence Hub is the primary experience. It is the Executive Briefing Room. The other 6 experiences are deeper intelligence environments. The 75+ screens that exist today remain — they are accessible through progressive disclosure within each experience, not as navigation items. This reduces cognitive load from 76 choices to 7, with depth available on demand.
