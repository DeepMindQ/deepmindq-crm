# Battle Card: Point Solutions

## Competitor Profile
Single-function intelligence tools: enrichment-only (Clearbit, ZoomInfo),
scoring-only (6sense), technographic-only (BuiltWith, Wappalyzer),
intent-data-only (Bombora, G2).

## DeepMindQ Position
Point solutions do one thing well. DeepMindQ does EVERYTHING and fuses
it into a single, confidence-graded intelligence picture. Instead of
paying for 5 point solutions and manually correlating their outputs,
you get one platform that does it all.

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Unified Intelligence Platform
- **We**: 7+ connectors → unified scoring → explainable recommendations.
  Signals from Crunchbase, SEC filings, website, RSS, LinkedIn, and
  file imports all flow into one engine.
- **Point Solutions**: Each tool provides isolated data. YOU do the
  correlation manually.

### 2. Intelligence Fusion Score
- **We**: Novel scoring that measures SOURCE AGREEMENT and DIVERSITY.
  When Crunchbase AND SEC filings AND LinkedIn all indicate the same
  signal, the fusion score is high — multi-source corroboration.
- **Point Solutions**: Single-source scores with no cross-validation.

### 3. Explainability Built In
- **We**: Every recommendation shows WHY — evidence provenance, confidence
  breakdown, risk factors, data depth indicator.
- **Point Solutions**: Raw data or opaque scores.

### 4. Adaptive Intelligence
- **We**: Our engine handles ANY company size. For Google, we analyze
  SEC filings and tech stack. For Joe's Plumbing, we analyze local signals
  and web presence. The intelligence adapts.
- **Point Solutions**: One-size-fits-all models, often biased toward
  mid-market+ companies with rich data profiles.

### 5. Hallucination Prevention
- **We**: Second-pass LLM verification catches AI-generated inaccuracies
  BEFORE they reach your sales team.
- **Point Solutions**: Limited or no hallucination detection.
