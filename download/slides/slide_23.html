MS6 is the foundation. MS7–MS11 are the building. A strong foundation determines the height of the building.

After this deck is approved, we proceed through three phases. Phase 1 produces the supporting documentation — the detailed specs behind the strategic decisions in this deck. Phase 2 builds the design system foundation — unified tokens, component library, and interaction patterns. Phase 3 prepares for MS7 — wireframes, validated layouts, and the development plan. MS6 does not produce production code. MS6 produces the design DNA that makes production code consistent.
