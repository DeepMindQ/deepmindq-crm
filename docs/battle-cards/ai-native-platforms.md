# Battle Card: AI-Native Sales Platforms

## Competitor Profile
AI-first sales intelligence and engagement platforms that combine data
enrichment with AI-driven outreach. Examples: Apollo.io, Clari, Gong,
Outreach, Salesloft.

## DeepMindQ Position
DeepMindQ is NOT an outreach tool. We are an intelligence-first platform
that helps sales teams UNDERSTAND their targets before engaging. Our AI
analyzes external data about prospect companies to surface timing, fit,
and capability alignment — the "when, why, and how" — not just the "who."

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Universal Company Coverage
- **We cover**: Google to Joe's Plumbing. Our intelligence engine adapts
  its analysis to ANY company size, from Fortune 500 to SMB.
- **Competitors**: Often optimized for mid-market+ only, miss SMB signals
  or produce low-quality scores for small companies.

### 2. Multi-Source Intelligence Fusion (Phase 2+3)
- **We**: Aggregate signals from 7+ data connectors (Crunchbase, SEC EDGAR,
  LinkedIn, RSS, website scraping, CSV/Excel imports, Clearbit) and fuse
  them into a single confidence-graded intelligence picture.
- **Competitors**: Typically rely on 1-2 data sources (often proprietary
  databases), creating single-source blind spots.

### 3. Explainability & Trust Architecture
- **We**: Every recommendation includes a full intelligence trail —
  evidence provenance, confidence breakdown by 6 dimensions, risk factors,
  and decision audit hashes for reproducibility.
- **Competitors**: Black-box scoring. "Trust our AI" without transparency.

### 4. Confidence Calibration Engine
- **We**: Track predicted vs. actual outcomes over time. Our scoring
  self-corrects based on real feedback loops (Phase 3).
- **Competitors**: Static scoring models that don't learn from outcomes.

### 5. Data Depth Indicators
- **We**: Every recommendation is labeled comprehensive/moderate/limited/minimal
  based on how much intelligence we actually have. We tell you when we DON'T
  know something.
- **Competitors**: Present all scores with equal confidence, regardless of
  underlying data quality.

## Proof Points
- 7 intelligence dimensions tracked per company
- 6-dimension confidence scoring (data quality, recency, coverage, etc.)
- Hallucination detection with second-pass LLM verification
- Multi-tenant scoring isolation for enterprise compliance
- pgvector-powered semantic search at scale
